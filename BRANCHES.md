# Multi-branch operations

Owners can create store locations from **Branches**, then assign each manager and cashier to a branch from **Users & Accounts**.

Branch staff are limited to their assigned branch's orders, shifts, stock balances, inventory movements, notifications, and reports. Product and recipe catalogs remain shared. Existing store data is assigned to the initial **Main Branch** by the branch-scoping migration.

The sales report defaults to combined sales across all branches and includes a per-branch breakdown. Owners can filter the report to one branch; branch managers are automatically limited to their assigned location. Inventory and stock reports can be filtered to a branch by owners.
